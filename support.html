Teachers Pet Support

For help with Teachers Pet, account connection questions, privacy requests, or deletion requests, contact the application owner through the email address associated with the Teachers Pet Google OAuth consent screen.

Disconnect Google access

You can revoke Teachers Pet's access at any time from your Google Account under Security → Connections to third-party apps and services.

Data deletion

After revoking access, you may also remove locally stored Teachers Pet application data from the device where the app is installed. For assistance locating or deleting application data, contact support.
